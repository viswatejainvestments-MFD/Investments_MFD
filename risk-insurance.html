The Problem

Why India's middle class
is one crisis away from ruin.

Insurance in India is widely misunderstood, massively underused, and often bought for the wrong reasons. Here is the reality.

💀
Critically underinsured life cover
The rule of thumb: life cover should be 15–20× your annual income. Most Indians have cover worth less than 3×. If you earn ₹15L/year, you need ₹2.25–3 Cr in term cover — not ₹50L.
🏥
Corporate cover is not enough
A ₹3L group health policy feels adequate — until a serious illness hits. Cancer, cardiac surgery, or organ transplant can cost ₹15–40L. Your corporate cover vanishes within days.
📋
ULIPs and endowment plans
Millions of Indians hold insurance-investment combo products that give terrible returns AND inadequate cover. We identify and help you restructure these efficiently.
🦽
No disability or CI cover
A critical illness or permanent disability can be financially more devastating than death — you're still alive, still incurring expenses, but your income has stopped. Almost nobody covers for this.
👨‍👩‍👧
Dependants fully exposed
If you are the primary earner and something happens to you, what happens to your family? Without adequate life and income cover, they are entirely vulnerable.
🔍
No holistic risk audit
Most people buy policies in isolation — one policy here, another there — with no systematic assessment of total risk exposure vs. total coverage. The gaps are invisible until disaster strikes.
