The analytics engine

This isn't a tracker.
It's an analyst.

Every night, yours re-runs six checks against your entire portfolio — the same checks a good advisor would do manually, if they had the time.

01 / TRUE XIRR
One number, every platform.
Every top-up, switch, dividend, and withdrawal across every AMC and broker, folded into a single money-weighted return — benchmarked against the Nifty 50 automatically.

How it works
