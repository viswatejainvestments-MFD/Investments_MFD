The Problem

Why generational wealth
is lost — not transferred.

Most families spend a lifetime accumulating wealth, only to see it dissipated by avoidable legal battles, tax leakage, and lack of planning. Here is what goes wrong.

📜
No Will — the most expensive mistake
Dying intestate (without a Will) means the state decides how your assets are distributed. The Hindu Succession Act doesn't reflect your personal wishes — it reflects a fixed legal formula.
🏛️
Probate delays and court battles
Without proper estate documentation, your family could spend 2–10 years in court just to access the assets you worked 30 years to build. Legal fees and family strain compound the loss.
📋
Wrong or missing nominations
Nomination ≠ inheritance. A nominee is a trustee, not the legal owner. Without a Will aligned to your nominations, your assets may go to someone you didn't intend to benefit.
👶
No guardian plan for minor children
If both parents pass away, who raises your children? Without a Will that names a guardian, the court decides. Your children's future is too important to leave to chance.
🏢
Business succession gaps
What happens to your business if you're gone? Without a clear succession plan, partnerships dissolve, businesses are liquidated below value, and employees lose livelihoods.
🔒
Digital assets are invisible
Demat accounts, crypto holdings, mutual fund folios, ULIPs — without a documented digital asset inventory and access plan, these simply disappear at death. No one knows they exist.
Our Approach

A complete legacy plan
built to last generations.

We take a legal, financial, and personal approach to estate planning — combining document structuring, asset mapping, and beneficiary strategy into one cohesive plan.

01
Complete Asset Inventory
We document all your assets — immovable property, financial instruments, business interests, digital assets, insurance policies, and jewellery — with current valuations and ownership structures.
02
Will Drafting & Legalisation
A clearly drafted, witnessed, and registered Will that reflects your exact wishes — beneficiary allocations, guardian appointments, conditional gifts, and executor nomination.
03
Nomination Alignment
We audit and update all nominations across every financial account, demat, mutual fund, insurance policy, and PF account — and align them with your Will to eliminate conflicts.
04
Trust & Succession Structuring
For HNI families and business owners — Private Family Trusts, business succession agreements, and multi-generational wealth structures that protect, grow, and distribute assets efficiently.
